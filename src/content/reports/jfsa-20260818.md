## What it is

The Japan Financial Services Agency (JFSA) is Japan's integrated financial regulator,  overseeing banks, securities firms, insurance, and the crypto-asset framework under the Payment Services Act and Financial Instruments and Exchange Act. For the tokenized settlement layer, JFSA is the Asia-Pacific rules node alongside the Monetary Authority of Singapore and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority: exchange licensing, stablecoin policy, and custody standards for yen and cross-border digital-asset settlement.
