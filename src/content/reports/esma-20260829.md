## What it is

The European Securities and Markets Authority is the EU's securities regulator and the primary implementation authority for MiCA. ESMA writes the technical standards that operationalize MiCA's framework,  custody, disclosure, market abuse rules,  and supervises CASP licensing via national competent authority coordination. For the tokenized settlement layer, ESMA's technical standards determine the practical compliance burden for EU-registered tokenized asset issuers and custodians, and its DLT Pilot oversight is the most advanced real-world test of blockchain settlement at institutional scale.
